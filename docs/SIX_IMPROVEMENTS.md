## Laptop report interpretation

The native test records the selected renderer and the actual CPU; the requested target label alone is not hardware evidence. A software renderer, headless run, undeclared power state or sample shorter than 60 seconds cannot establish native laptop acceptance. Keep the test window foregrounded, use normal thermal/power settings and compare AC and battery runs.

Prism's late-run fixture uses a validated save at maximum normal-mode speed. Flight chooses the greatest submitted geometry among twelve seeded position/yaw samples, then exercises boost/turns and sector streaming. These are declared scenarios, not proof of the worst possible scene. All modes and full-console/manual play remain necessary for complete acceptance.

Entry/exit heap samples are collected after explicit browser GC. Renderer counters can retain internal caches after disposal; a nonzero counter alone does not prove a leak. Review trends after warm-up and use platform heap/process/GPU tools for retained-memory or power conclusions. The tool does not certify enjoyment, physical input latency or visual quality.
