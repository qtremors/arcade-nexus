# Manual acceptance and playtest

Automated checks cannot establish enjoyment, native GPU performance or physical-device compatibility. Use a fresh throwaway profile, export before testing an existing household, and record browser/OS/controller/display refresh rate.

1. After installing dependencies, disconnect Internet (keep loopback available), launch `python arcade.py`, and play all eight titles. Stop/restart and verify players, favorites and saves remain.
2. Use only a controller to create a player with the on-screen keyboard, browse Games, start, pause, save, resume and quit. Unplug/reconnect during play. Verify no stuck motion or action leaks. Check a second controller model and Bluetooth/wired paths.
3. Abruptly terminate the browser during an active run, reopen the same origin, and Continue. Compare to the latest five-second snapshot. Repeat while the server is temporarily stopped; restart it and retry pending results.
4. Export, import as copies, and check restored slots, records, awards and preference values. Keep the original player and verify their data did not change.
5. Play all three Breakout sectors honestly. Record reaction difficulty, last-brick cleanup time, aiming fairness and earned medal. Compare easy/normal/hard records. Then try all six Arcade sectors: catch W/M pickups, check expiry and multiball life loss. Verify armor, explosive bricks, and cleanup assistance are understandable.
6. Complete a five-ring race in Stellar Flight, then deliberately miss gates and collide. Confirm the journey continues, progression persists, and new routes remain available. Test analog steering, ship/camera switching, and survey exploration. Try all four route patterns, identify survey landmarks, change contracts with H/L3, and compare ship boost/shield tradeoffs. Check gate readability against all backgrounds.
7. In 2048, lose with undo available; recover and continue. Reach 2048; keep playing. Compare challenge mode. Repeat a move after undo and check the identical spawn. Solve daily puzzles using only the board reader and keyboard; verify live announcements with a screen reader. Check all Snake mazes and optional bonus expiry, and Raptor phrases/biomes at each difficulty.
8. Test keyboard-only menus, 200% zoom, 800×600, system reduced motion, sound disabled, and visible focus. Check table sorting and modal focus returns.
9. Observe a new player without coaching for ten minutes. Note time to first play, objective comprehension, first failure, whether they restart voluntarily, confusion, frustration and comments about fairness. Do not label balance finished based solely on automated tests.
10. On named integrated-graphics laptops and dedicated-GPU PCs, capture cold launch and warm replay, frame p50/p95 (Settings export), browser heap snapshots after ten entry/restart/exit cycles, and input-to-visible-response video. Compare 60/120/144 Hz displays while confirming rendering stays limited to 60 FPS. Target sustained 60 FPS at 1080p with medium-or-better graphics. Capture at least 60 seconds per game/mode, including late-game load, and report average FPS plus p95 frame time (16.7 ms budget at 60 Hz). Record the selected GPU, driver, power mode, AC/battery state, thermals, and CPU/GPU utilization/power. Check pause/focus loss stops rendering. Verify culling while turning, moving between sectors and changing Flight cameras, and cache invalidation after Snake maze/Breakout armor/stage/save changes. See PERFORMANCE.md for scope and reproduction.

Record observations and concrete follow-ups in TASKS.md. There is no CI/CD requirement.

## Target laptop and follow-up checks

The requested target is a 12th-generation Intel i7 without a dedicated GPU. Record the full CPU SKU, actual selected integrated adapter, driver, RAM configuration, browser/OS, display refresh/scaling and power mode. Do not infer an Iris Xe/UHD model or achievable FPS from the i7 name alone.

After setup, run `python scripts/laptop_check.py --power ac` on that machine, keeping the window visible and competing heavy apps closed. Edge can use `--browser msedge`; Chromium can use `--browser chromium` or `CHROMIUM_PATH`. Repeat with `--power battery` and `--all-modes`. The tool adds declared late-run 3D scenarios, captures 60 seconds per case and measures ten reentries by default. Review actual GPU identity, FPS/p95, resets, CPU/GPU platform measurements and heap/resource trends together. Its isolated engine fixture leaves player data untouched and supplements the full-console/manual matrix above.

- With the console visible, verify empty result queues produce no retry activity; hide the tab and confirm connection checks/controller menu polling stop. Return and check recovery, held buttons, and pending sync. Compare battery use with platform tools before attributing a battery-life gain.
- Create a manual automatic-backup snapshot, inspect its preview, cancel, then restore copies. Confirm originals remain and browser preferences stay unchanged. Download the portable file and exercise normal file preview/import. Test a write-protected/full backup folder and verify the previous copy remains available with a visible error.
- Make more than 25 sessions and verify an older best appears first when sorting by score. Combine game/mode/difficulty/local-date filters and check counts/page reset. Repeat around a local midnight/DST boundary.
- Try assigning a default alias, another custom binding, Pause or a secondary shortcut to movement. Confirm the conflict stays visible without changing the control, choose a valid key, reload, then reset controls. Test real wired and Bluetooth controllers, reconnects and buttons held while returning from another tab.
- At actual 200% browser zoom and with a screen reader, inspect history filters, backup previews, recovery actions and controller onboarding. Automated contrast/semantics checks cover six shell pages; they do not provide complete nonvisual canvas gameplay or replace assistive-technology users.

## New game acceptance

- Tetris: clear one through four rows, try rotations against walls/floor, hold twice before locking, and finish a 40-line sprint. Save mid-piece and verify the stack, hold, next sequence, and score on Continue. Check fast left/right repeats on keyboard and controller. Confirm only completed sprints establish time records, faster runs beat higher-scoring slower runs, and previous rules remain separate.
- Asteroids: coast without thrust, brake, fire across a wrapped edge, split each rock size, lose all three hulls, and reach the next wave. Save with bullets and fragments in flight. Confirm respawn protection is readable.
- Prism Rush: take every lane, cross from far left to far right between gates, brake at maximum speed, collect crystals, and lose all shields. Check the HUD at 800×600, resize/fullscreen, and reduced motion. Save while turning and return repeatedly; verify HUD and GPU resources do not accumulate.
